The New Rules

Three Rules for the Agentic Security Era

The old security playbook assumed humans in the loop. These rules don’t.

01

The binary is ground truth

Every artifact your AI generates is a security concern — from creation to deployment. The binary, not source code, is the source of truth.

02

Trust must be computed

Governance must be native — built where your artifacts live and enforced at the point of request. Dashboards can’t keep up at machine speed.

03

Artifacts carry their own proof

Every artifact must be self-describing — carrying its compliance record, provenance, and cryptographic attestations wherever it goes.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Shadow AI occurs when developers and teams adopt AI tools, models, and third-party ML assets without centralized visibility — faster than security teams can track. JFrog AI Catalog solves this with a single control plane that provides complete visibility across all AI assets in the organization.
JFrog MCP Registry with Secure Gateway acts as a supply chain firewall for AI agents, blocking connections to unverified MCP servers and routing coding agents through a secure gateway with granular access controls and a complete audit trail.
Agentic remediation uses contextual AI to identify which vulnerabilities are actually reachable and exploitable in your running application — then automatically generates the remediation code to fix them, cutting through the noise of thousands of undifferentiated CVE alerts.
ML models are executable binaries that run code the moment they load into memory, yet most organizations treat them as untouchable data with no versioning, no lineage tracking, and no security controls — making them one of the most dangerous unguarded attack surfaces in the modern software supply chain.
The JFrog Platform enforces security natively at the artifact level — directly where your binaries and models live — rather than relying on a decoupled ASPM dashboard overlay that creates a structural latency gap between your artifacts and your security evidence.
