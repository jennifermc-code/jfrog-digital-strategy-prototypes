# Executive Communication

## Quick Start

When asked to create executive communications:

1. Identify the format (QBR, executive summary, stakeholder update, presentation)
2. Determine the audience (C-suite, VP, cross-functional, board)
3. Gather the data and key messages
4. Apply the appropriate template below
5. Ensure the "so what" is clear in every section

## Core Principles

- **Lead with insight, not data** — Every chart needs a headline that states the takeaway
- **Pyramid structure** — Most important conclusion first, supporting details after
- **Action-oriented** — Every section should make clear what decisions are needed
- **Appropriate depth** — C-suite gets headlines; VPs get detail; ICs get methodology

## QBR (Quarterly Business Review)

For the full QBR template, see [qbr-template.md](qbr-template.md).

### QBR Structure
1. **Executive Summary** (1 slide / 1 paragraph) — Quarter in 3 sentences
2. **Scorecard** — KPIs vs. targets with red/yellow/green status
3. **Wins** — Top 3-5 accomplishments with quantified impact
4. **Challenges** — Honest assessment of what didn't work and lessons learned
5. **Key Initiatives Deep Dive** — 1-2 slides per major initiative with results
6. **Next Quarter Plan** — Priorities, resource needs, expected outcomes
7. **Asks** — Specific decisions or approvals needed from leadership

### Scorecard Format
| KPI | Target | Actual | Status | Trend | Commentary |
|-----|--------|--------|--------|-------|------------|
| | | | 🟢/🟡/🔴 | ↑/→/↓ | |

## Executive Summary Framework

For any analysis, project update, or recommendation:

```markdown
# [Title]

## TL;DR
[2-3 sentences: situation, key finding, recommended action]

## Context
[Why this matters now — 2-3 sentences max]

## Key Data Points
- [Metric]: [Value] ([comparison])
- [Metric]: [Value] ([comparison])

## Recommendation
[Clear, specific recommended action]

## Impact if We Act
[Quantified expected outcome]

## Impact if We Don't Act
[Risk or cost of inaction]

## Next Steps
| Action | Owner | Due Date |
|--------|-------|----------|
```

## Data Storytelling

When presenting data to leadership:

### Narrative Arc
1. **Setup** — "Here's the question we set out to answer"
2. **Tension** — "Here's what the data revealed (including surprises)"
3. **Resolution** — "Here's what it means and what we should do"

### Chart Headline Rules
- Every chart gets an insight headline (not a description)
- Bad: "Monthly Traffic by Channel"
- Good: "Organic traffic grew 23% while paid declined — shifting our channel mix"

### Number Presentation
- Use comparisons: "up 15% vs. last quarter" not just "15%"
- Use context: "$50K savings" means more than "12% reduction"
- Round appropriately: "~2.3M sessions" not "2,314,587 sessions"
- Lead with the most impactful number

## Presentation Tips

- **10/20/30 rule**: Max 10 slides, 20 minutes, 30pt minimum font
- **One idea per slide** — If you need two points, use two slides
- **Appendix is your friend** — Detail goes in backup slides, not the main flow
- **Rehearse the transitions** — "This matters because..." between every slide
